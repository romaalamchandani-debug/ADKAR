01 — The idea

One change.
Five human needs.

ADKAR focuses on the individual experience of change. Instead of asking only, “Did we launch the initiative?”, ask, “What does this person need to move forward?”

A · D · K · A · R
Core idea: people move through change one stage at a time. A gap in any stage can slow or stop adoption.

The sequence matters.

Training can teach the how.

It cannot create the why.
08 — A useful distinction

Training is only part of the change.

Training typically supports Knowledge and Ability. People also need context and motivation to engage with the change — and reinforcement afterward to sustain it.

5different outcomes can become five different barriers. Diagnose the barrier before choosing the intervention.
